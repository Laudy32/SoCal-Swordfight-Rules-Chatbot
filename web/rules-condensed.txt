SOCAL SWORDFIGHT 2026 — CONDENSED RULES REFERENCE
(This is a shortened summary of the official ruleset for quick lookup. It omits
some fine detail, especially exact cutting-tournament choreography and judge
hand-signal mechanics. For anything not clearly covered here, or anything
high-stakes (a disputed call, a safety question, an equipment approval), say
so plainly and point the person to a tournament director or staff member
rather than guessing.)

== CODE OF CONDUCT ==
SoCal Swordfight is dedicated to a safe, respectful, inclusive environment.
Violating the code of conduct can mean expulsion without refund and a ban
from future events. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated (offensive
language about gender, sexual orientation, race, ethnicity, disability,
religion, physical appearance; intimidation, stalking, inappropriate
physical contact, unwelcome sexual attention). Safety violations include
severely injuring someone through an intentional act, or ignoring safety
protocols from staff/instructors/vendors. Report violations immediately to
lead staff or anyone wearing a SoCal Swordfight volunteer shirt.

== GENERAL TOURNAMENT RULES (apply to all tournaments) ==
Goals: good sportsmanship, competitive skill development, historical
technique in a modern context, respect for sharp-sword safety.

Forbidden actions (all tournaments):
- Unrestrained-force strikes; a fighter must try to arrest a strike in
  progress if safety is at risk.
- High-amplitude throws/leg locks/wraps (raising hips above head, or
  accelerating opponent to the ground) — only allowed where a specific
  weapon's rules say so.
- Ankle/foot is always an illegal target.
- Exposing the spine/back of the head to an opponent (still your job to
  hold back the strike even if they expose it).
- Striking with anything but the sword (punches, elbows, knees, headbutts).
- Any dangerous action (diving/throwing yourself at the ground, etc).
- No hilt attacks, no throwing objects.

Ring outs: both feet leaving the ring = hold called, -1 point (non-safety
penalty). Leaving the ring area fast/dangerously = safety penalty. Any
action by a fighter who rings themself out doesn't count (their hits are
voided; if they get hit while ringing out, the opponent still scores).

Match conduct: fighters must act with skill, control, professionalism, and
follow staff instructions. The director is the ultimate ring authority.
Discipline generally escalates: yellow card (warning) → red card (point
deduction) → expulsion — director's discretion, can skip steps for severity.
Self-calls: a fighter may voluntarily tell the director they were hit, hit
in a higher-value target than judges saw, missed, or hit a lower-value
target — only ever in a way that helps their opponent, never themselves.

Penalties: Safety penalties escalate — Yellow Card (warning) → Red Card
(-3 points) → Black Card (match forfeit) → expulsion; escalation can be
based on a fighter's prior safety record across the whole event. Non-safety
penalties (White Cards) are a flat -1 point, no escalation. A penalized
fighter's scoring actions in that exchange are voided.

Promptness: be ready (queued, geared up) when your match is called, or take
a penalty.

Match length: matches go to a 7-point cap. Clock is stopped, not running
continuously. If nobody hits the cap by 90 seconds, overtime begins — a
leading fighter wins on their next point; a trailing fighter can still come
back. At 150 seconds (2.5 min) total, hard cap hits and the higher score
wins outright.

Scoring action quality (cuts/thrusts/slices/pommel strikes):
- Cuts need real cutting plane (edge aligned to the direction of travel),
  body structure (body/sword connected at impact), and blade rotation
  (usually 60°+) — hard impact alone isn't enough.
- Thrusts need real penetration (roughly 3"+), shown by blade flex, a
  visible force reaction, or a fighter deliberately shortening their
  extension to control force. Light touches or "chasing" with the point
  aren't valid.
- Slices count only against the upper opening/arm, need a clear force
  reaction (pushing the target, restricting movement) — no back-and-forth
  sawing without real force transfer.
- Pommel strikes: front-of-mask only; must be a deliberate, controlled,
  in-line motion (not a wild swinging hilt); don't need to land if the path
  to target is clearly unobstructed.

Targets: "Upper opening" = head + top of torso (shoulder seam to shoulder
seam). Arm strikes include hand strikes (hitting the grip counts as a hand
hit). Torso = above the waist besides upper opening; leg = below the waist
(roughly the jacket line). Foot/ankle is always illegal. Unclear target
calls default to the lower-value target.

Double hits: both fighters land valid, quality actions "in flight" at the
same time (doesn't require simultaneous landing, just overlapping motion).
Scored as no points for either side, with an increasing per-double
deduction over a pool (-1/-2/-3...). Three doubles in one pool match ends
it with no winner. A double that's clearly caused by one fighter ignoring
an obvious threat gets that fighter an extra penalty.

Afterblows: a valid hit landing as the very next action after a clean
(non-double) hit — reduces the initial attacker's score by 1 point. Any
intervening action, step, or feint breaks the afterblow window.

Pulled attacks generally don't score — you have to actually strike to get
points — except when pulling back was necessary for safety (e.g. opponent
fell, or exposed the back of the head); in that case the fighter who safely
arrested their strike is awarded points instead.

Equipment (baseline for all tournaments, weapon-specific rules add more):
No heavy/fantasy armor, no dangling decorations on gear (bells, streamers,
etc — these can disqualify equipment at staff discretion). All gear and
weapons are inspected before competing. Required: back-of-head-covering
mask, throat/trachea protection, groin protection (testicular protection
required, general groin protection recommended), hard elbow protection
(covering all 3 points), hard knee protection, no exposed skin (including
shins). Gloves/jacket/shin-forearm specifics vary by weapon — see below.

== WEAPON-SPECIFIC RULES ==
(All weapon tournaments also follow the General Tournament Rules above.)

LONGSWORD
- Open Longsword requires a cutting qualification (pre-qualified if you
  passed a cutting qualification or placed in a cutting tournament in
  2024/2025 — check your roster page to confirm). Otherwise attend the
  Friday cutting qualification (unlimited retries): 4 required cut types
  (clean descending cut right, clean descending cut left, arrested
  "half-cut" right, arrested half-cut left) across 6 attempts.
- Matches to 7 points. Scoring: 3 pts for cut/thrust to upper opening or
  thrust to torso (standing takedown also 3 pts, Tier-A/B Open & URG
  Tier-A only); 1 pt for all other cuts/thrusts, slices to upper
  opening/arm, or pommel strike to mask. +1 extra point for a "Bound
  Action" (your blade in contact with the opponent's blade when the hit
  lands, or their blade already controlled by a grapple).
- One-handed strikes are illegal outside of grappling.
- Throws/takedowns only allowed in Open Tier-A/B and URG Tier-A.
- Required gear beyond baseline: purpose-built heavy HEMA gloves (no
  lacrosse/Red Dragon-style gloves), heavy padded HEMA jacket, shin
  protection required, forearm protection recommended.
- Sword specs: blade 84-102cm (33-40"), overall <140cm (55"), mass
  1.3-1.8kg (2.9-3.9 lbs), from a HEMA manufacturer.

RAPIER (Single Rapier or Rapier & Dagger)
- Matches to 7 points. Scoring: 3 pts thrust to head/torso; 1 pt thrust to
  other targets or a cut (dagger cuts only score against the head).
- All body wrestling (throws, joint locks, pushing out of bounds) is
  forbidden. Blade grabs of a stationary blade allowed but must release
  after one action (extra holding = -1 pt penalty). No pommel/hilt
  strikes; no twisting an opponent's hilt.
- Required gear beyond baseline: leather fencing gloves (weapon hand),
  light padded glove for an empty off-hand, purpose-built fencing torso
  garment. Shin protection recommended, not required.
- Sword specs: blade <45", overall <51", mass 0.6-1.4kg (1.3-3.1 lbs).
  Dagger: straight blade only (no swordbreakers/trident daggers), blade
  <18", overall <24". Bright contrasting tip color required on both.

SABER (incl. U18 Singlestick)
- Matches to 7 points. Scoring: 3 pts cut/thrust to upper opening or
  thrust to torso; 1 pt other cuts/thrusts, slices to upper opening/arm,
  or standing takedown. Last 1/3 of blade counts as a valid (false-edge)
  cutting surface. U18 Singlestick: thrusting is not allowed/not scored.
- Throws/takedowns only in Tier A/B Saber (Open & URG); not in Tier C or
  U18 Singlestick.
- Required gear beyond baseline: heavy HEMA gloves (no lacrosse/Red
  Dragon), heavy padded jacket, shin protection required, forearm
  protection recommended.
- Weapon: 1796 Blackfencer Infantry Saber is provided for the tournament.

SMALLSWORD
- Single-exchange matches: ends on an unanswered thrust, a double/
  afterblow, or 60 seconds elapsed (counts as a double). Ranking uses
  hits-scored ÷ times-hit.
- Body wrestling forbidden; blade grabs of a stationary blade must release
  after one action.
- Required gear beyond baseline: leather fencing gloves, protective torso
  garment (thrust-resistant). Unlike other weapons, hard elbow and knee
  protection are NOT required for smallsword.
- Weapon: bring your own, blade 30-35", triangular or schlaeger style,
  18th-century historical grip/guard only (no epee guards, pistol grips,
  or foil-style blades). Bright contrasting tip color required.

CASTILLE ARMORY DUAL SIDESWORD
- Short matches: 5 scoring exchanges or 2 minutes stopped-clock. Scoring
  is "fully weighted doubles" — afterblows aren't scored; if both land,
  each scores their own hit's value. 2 pts cut/thrust to upper opening;
  1 pt other thrusts or cuts to arm/knee/shin; cuts to torso/thigh don't
  score, and hitting with both swords at once doesn't score. Advancement
  score = points scored minus times hit.
- Required gear beyond baseline: heavy HEMA gloves, heavy padded jacket,
  shin protection required, forearm protection recommended.
- Weapon: Castille Armory sideswords are provided (34" blade, single ring
  hilt, no knucklebow/finger rings).

== CUTTING TOURNAMENTS (Longsword & Arming Sword) ==
(A SEPARATE competition from sparring — sharp swords cutting a tatami/paper
target, not an opponent. "Cut" here means a judged cutting-tournament
attempt, NOT a sparring scoring action — do not mix this section into
sparring/match-scoring questions or vice versa.)
Goal: test cutting mechanics that can't be evaluated in sparring.
Safety: follow staff direction exactly, never swing near others, dry
hands/hilt, don't touch mats without instruction, don't cut before staff
signal. Arming sword: keep the off-hand back, don't extend it forward.
No cutting toward your lead leg with descending cuts, except Tier-A cutting
which allows any direction.
Equipment: European longswords/arming swords only (loaner swords
available); no messers, sabers, zweihanders, etc without prior approval.
Judging: each cut is scored out of 10, combining a cut-quality deduction
and a form deduction (roughly: Final Score = 10 − sqrt(cutDeduction² +
formDeduction²)). Cut deductions cover things like "mountains" (blade
re-crossing a previous cut), landing zone/distance of the cut piece,
scallops (curved cut path), cutting below the marked limit, or missing/
wrong cuts. Form deductions cover chambering errors, balance issues, and
(in reactive rounds) not being in the waiting area at the right time.
Hitting the cutting stand or floor, or releasing the sword, are penalized
severely (can be a DQ). Paper-target cuts use a similar but separate
deduction scale.
The exact cutting patterns/order required differ by weapon and
tier (Tier A/B/C, U18) and get fairly detailed round-by-round — ask a
tournament director for the specific pattern required for your division
rather than relying on this summary.
Competitor conduct: wait for staff signal before cutting; wait for the
judges' color-coded card (green = no deduction, yellow = deduction,
red = no points, verbal = penalty) before your next cut; don't argue a
score with the table; don't take excessive time once signaled to cut.

== AGGREGATE TEAM AWARDS ==
Rewards schools/teams for both top performance and overall depth. Each
competitor's best individual result counts toward their team: finalist
(top 4) = 9 pts, top 1/3 of a tournament = 3 pts, top 1/2 = 1 pt. Team
score is the sum of every member's best contribution (each person counted
once, even with multiple medals). All tournaments except experimental and
smallsword count toward the aggregate award.

== HOW JUDGING/DIRECTING WORKS (background, staff-facing material) ==
A match has one director and (usually) three line judges. After each
exchange, judges independently signal what they saw (clean hit, double
hit, no action, afterblow, or abstain) and the target area. The director
generally follows the judges' majority call but has discretion (mainly
for quality/safety questions), and can hold a brief private conference if
judges disagree before making the final ruling. Anyone can call "Hold" or
"Safety" for a safety concern, and "MEDIC" immediately for a medical need.
